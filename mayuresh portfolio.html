    Nexqit

    A multimodal journey planner · Aug 2025 – Present

    Nexqit is my mobility-tech startup. It is a unified journey-planning app that brings bus, metro and rideshare together with real-time routing, booking APIs and payments, so people can plan and book a trip in one place.

    The Problem

    Planning a single journey means switching between separate bus, metro and rideshare apps, each with its own routes, bookings and payments.

    The Vision

    One unified multimodal journey-planning experience, from route to booking to payment.

    The Solution

    Nexqit brings bus, metro and rideshare together with real-time routing, booking APIs and payment infrastructure in one app.
